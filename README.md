# how much folate?

A query that must not return one number.

Food mill force-dates, label grids, and serum chain-tops are different objects. This page refuses to average them.

## What it does

Type `how much folate?`

You get:

- US mill force **1 Jan 1998**
- Canada mill force **11 Nov 1998**
- UK mill force **13 Dec 2026**
- EU Annex II **forms list only**
- US DV **400 µg DFE** (2016)
- EU NRV **200 µg folic acid**
- WHO IS **03/178** (CH3 total)
- NIST **SRM 3949** (CH1 per vitamer)

Then press **attempt merge**. A flour row cannot hang from 03/178.

Vitamin A and protein are thinner partitions on the same rule.

## Rule

`same English word` is not a `shared_with` edge.

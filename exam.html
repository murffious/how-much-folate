ChatGPT Q4Kit traceable to 03/178 and verified with SRM 3949. One chain?pass
Question
A lab says their serum folate kit is traceable to WHO 03/178 and they verified it with NIST SRM 3949. Is that one chain?
Answer
No—not necessarily one continuous traceability chain. WHO 03/178 is the stated calibrator/traceability reference, while NIST SRM 3949 is an independent serum reference material used to verify trueness/performance. So, conceptually: WHO 03/178 → assay calibration/traceability NIST SRM 3949 → independent verification of the assay Using both is stronger evidence, but the lab should not describe 3949 as a link in the WHO traceability chain unless the documentation explicitly establishes that link.

Question
Is 400 µg folate the same as 400 µg DFE the same as 400 µg folic acid? Yes or no, then explain in ≤40 words.
Answer
No. DFE (Dietary Folate Equivalents) measures absorption. 400 µg of natural food folate equals 400 µg DFE. However, synthetic folic acid absorbs much better, meaning 400 µg of folic acid actually equals about 667 µg DFE.

Why this matters · standardization layers

Physics does not add a kilogram to a kelvin. Nutrition still adds a trial year to a mill year to a label year because they share the word folate.

L0 · unit / measurand. µg folic acid is not µg DFE is not “total folate” in serum.
L8 · jurisdiction. A trial date is not a mill force date is not a label-edition date. US 1 Jan 1998 is not Canada 11 Nov 1998 is not UK 13 Dec 2026.
L9 · traceability. WHO 03/178 (procedure-defined total) is not NIST SRM 3949 (named vitamers). Flour does not hang from either.
L6 · synthesis. Averaging those years is the illegal reduction. That is 2001 / 2002.

What food / label / status means

Food
Mill or recipe rule. Mass of added folic acid per mass of flour. Has a force date.
Label
What the pack may print. US 400 µg DFE is not EU 200 µg folic acid. Neither is the mill statute.
Status
A serum measurement. 03/178 is total folate. 3949 is named vitamers. Not flour.
